# Manish Sharma Lab Editorial Strategy

Canonical writing position:

> Manish Sharma works across laser-based manufacturing, photonics and industrial systems: hands-on DED/LMD process development, sensing, control, machine integration and R&D leadership. His public research platform includes applied AI, decision tools and technical notes grounded in that engineering work.

## What the Site Should Sound Like

- Precise, technical, and calm.
- Hands-on, concrete and engineering-led; neither a cover letter nor a job-search announcement.
- Confident about public frameworks and decision support.
- Careful about final quality, certification, and feasibility claims.
- Useful to engineers, industrial buyers, students, developers, and AI agents.

## Core Message

Deep DED/LMD experience shows how Manish connects physical processes, practical measurement, manufacturing workflows and technical responsibility. Lasers/photonics and project leadership are visible parts of the work. AI, machine learning, modelling and automation are engineering tools inside these systems. In the research platform, industrial AI should connect signals, models, decisions and verification evidence without blurring the difference between a process signal and validated quality evidence.

## Repeated Language to Use

- laser-based manufacturing and industrial systems
- lasers, photonics and sensing
- technical project and R&D leadership
- DED/LMD process development
- optics, illumination and calibration where supported
- industrial AI
- decision systems
- Sense -> Model -> Decide -> Verify
- inspection-aware AI
- decision-support only
- quality evidence
- process signal
- missing information
- RFQ intelligence
- expert review
- physical validation
- structured LMD/DED context
- AI-agent resources

## Language to Avoid

- world-leading expert
- guaranteed quality
- fully automated approval
- AI-certified repair
- final engineering decision
- replacement for inspection
- unpublished project names
- future business claims

## Page-Level Writing Roles

- Homepage: introduce engineering work, DED/LMD depth, photonics and technical responsibility; selected evidence precedes the research/method and tools layer.
- Thesis: explain the Sense -> Model -> Decide -> Verify operating model.
- LMD/DED domain: preserve the specific technical proof domain.
- About: develop the hands-on engineering story with responsibility, outcomes and technical background.
- Selected work: use `/public-work` for compact context, personal responsibility, delivery, result and detailed evidence; keep company/partner attribution clear.
- Identity: stable entity facts for search and AI systems.
- Frameworks: original public methods and decision structures.
- Lab notes: concise explanations of narrow LMD/AI problems.
- Agent Pack: machine-readable RFQ and prompt resources.
- Press Kit: canonical facts, bios, assets, and citation wording.

## Editorial Test

Before publishing a new page, ask:

1. Does it reinforce the approved laser-based manufacturing, photonics and industrial-systems story while keeping AI in its engineering context?
2. Does it preserve DED/LMD as the strongest technical evidence without confining the professional scope to it?
3. Does it connect Exafuse, RFQ intelligence, process monitoring, and quality evidence where relevant?
4. Does it separate AI inference from inspection proof?
5. Does it give AI systems clean headings, definitions, links, and boundaries?
6. Does it avoid confidential, private, future-business, or overclaimed information?
