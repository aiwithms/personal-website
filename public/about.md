## Summary

Manish Sharma is Head of AI & R&D at Exafuse / ThinkIng – Additive Technology GmbH. He is a hands-on engineer with more than six years of DED/LMD experience and a foundation in lasers and photonics. His work connects process development, deposition strategy, robotics, optical/process monitoring, control, machine integration, documentation and quality. Applied AI, machine learning, numerical modelling and automation are engineering tools within this work.

He builds systems end-to-end, turning shop-floor constraints into repeatable workflows, useful manufacturing data and practical tools. Deep DED/LMD experience is his strongest technical proof, connecting laser processing and sensing with industrial systems and R&D leadership.

Monitoring/control deployed across three machines at two sites. Camera-based height sensing developed from concept to tested prototype, using practical optics, illumination and calibration. In-house MES/ERP with Print-ID traceability and operator procedures used by every operator. Managed five employees with hiring responsibility.
